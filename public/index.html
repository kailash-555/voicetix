💡 Earphone & Microphone Tips:
• Auto-Switch: Connecting wired or Bluetooth earphones automatically routes your voice input.
• Earphone Mic: If Windows asks "Which device did you plug in?", choose Headset (with microphone).
• In Windows Settings > System > Sound > Input, make sure your headset/earphone is selected as the default input device.
• Always ensure microphone permission is Allowed in your browser URL bar.

📜 Booking Terms & Conditions

1. Admission & Digital Pass: All cinema attendees must present their digital QR pass at the entrance turnstile. Admission is strictly valid for the booked movie, cinema hall, date, and allocated seats.

2. Convenience Fees & GST: A non-refundable digital platform convenience fee of 10% plus 18% GST on the fee is applicable to all cinema seat reservations.

3. WhatsApp & 2-Factor OTP Verification: For payment security and fraud prevention, a 6-digit OTP is dispatched via WhatsApp to your registered mobile number prior to final payment verification.

4. Cancellation & Refunds: Cancellation is permitted up to 2 hours prior to scheduled showtime directly from "My Bookings". Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 24–48 hours.

5. WhatsApp Ticket Delivery Consent: By proceeding, you consent to receive security OTPs and confirmed cinema admission tickets with QR passes on your mobile number via WhatsApp.
